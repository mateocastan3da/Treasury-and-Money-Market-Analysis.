# Fixed Income Analytics

A collection of Python notebooks analyzing **money market rates, U.S. Treasury securities, and bond yields**.

The project applies fixed-income pricing and yield calculations to real market data, with a focus on understanding how short-term instruments and Treasury securities are quoted and evaluated.

## Topics Covered

* Money market rate calculations
* Treasury pricing and yield conventions
* Treasury-bill discount yields
* Bond yield analysis
* Maturity and price relationships
* Fixed-income market conventions

## Notebooks

### Money Market Rates

Analyzes short-term money market instruments and applies standard market-rate conventions.

### Treasury Analysis

Examines U.S. Treasury securities, including pricing, maturity, and yield calculations.

### Yield Analysis

Explores bond yields and the relationship between market prices, maturity, and implied returns.

## Technologies

* Python
* pandas
* NumPy
* Jupyter Notebook

## Purpose

This project was developed to apply fixed-income concepts programmatically and build familiarity with the pricing and yield conventions used across Treasury and money markets.
